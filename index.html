OTC 止痛藥：便利與風險的拉鋸 OTC Painkillers: The Tug-of-War Between Convenience and Risk

從市售止痛藥的藥理副作用，深度探討指示用藥（OTC）進駐便利商店之政策利弊與公衛影響。 An in-depth exploration of the pharmacological side effects of over-the-counter (OTC) painkillers and the public health implications of deregulating their sales in convenience stores.

市售止痛藥成分與副作用解析 Analysis of OTC Painkiller Ingredients and Side Effects

乙醯胺酚類 Acetaminophen

代表藥物：普拿疼 e.g., Panadol, Tylenol

作用機轉 (Mechanism)： 主要作用於中樞神經系統，阻斷疼痛傳導並具有退燒效果，但不具備消炎作用。 Acts primarily on the central nervous system to block pain transmission and reduce fever, but lacks anti-inflammatory properties.

  • 肝毒性風險 Hepatotoxicity Risk 由肝臟酵素代謝，若過量使用或併服酒精，易產生有毒代謝物 NAPQI，導致猛爆性肝炎。 Overdose or concurrent alcohol use can generate the toxic metabolite NAPQI, leading to fulminant hepatitis.
  • 潛在交互作用 Potential Interactions 隱藏於多種感冒糖漿與綜合感冒藥中，易在不知情下重複投藥導致過量。 Hidden in various cold syrups, leading to unintentional overdose through duplicated administration.

非類固醇消炎止痛藥 NSAIDs

代表藥物：布洛芬 e.g., Ibuprofen, Advil

作用機轉 (Mechanism)： 抑制環氧化酶 (COX)，阻斷前列腺素合成，同時具備止痛、退燒與抗發炎效果。 Inhibits cyclooxygenase (COX) to block prostaglandin synthesis, providing analgesic, antipyretic, and anti-inflammatory effects.

  • 腸胃道損害 Gastrointestinal Damage 減少胃部保護性前列腺素分泌，長期或空腹使用易引發潰瘍或出血。 Reduces protective gastric prostaglandins. Long-term use can induce ulcers or bleeding.
  • 腎臟與心血管風險 Renal & Cardiovascular Risks 影響腎臟血流可能導致腎功能衰退；部分 NSAIDs 會增加心血管事件風險。 Alters renal blood flow, potentially causing kidney decline and increasing cardiovascular risks.

通路鬆綁：便利商店販售 OTC 藥物之思辨 Deregulation: The Debate on Selling OTC Drugs in Convenience Stores

支持開放
Pro-Deregulation

落實自我照護 Empowering Self-Care 賦權民眾自行處理輕微且短暫的疼痛，可減少輕症湧入急診。 Empowering the public to manage mild, temporary pain reduces non-urgent emergency room visits.
購藥可近性 Enhanced Accessibility 便利商店 24 小時營業特性，能填補夜間或假日藥局打烊時的醫療需求。 The 24/7 nature of convenience stores fulfills the need for immediate medical relief during nights.

反對開放
Anti-Deregulation

缺乏專業把關 Lack of Professional Gatekeeping 店員無藥學專業，無法評估消費者用藥史，易導致嚴重的藥物交互作用。 Store clerks lack pharmaceutical expertise to evaluate a consumer's medical history.
過量與濫用風險 Risk of Overdose and Abuse 若隨意將 OTC 與感冒藥混吃，極易突破安全劑量造成不可逆的肝損傷。 Mixing OTC painkillers with cold medicines can easily cause irreversible liver damage.
